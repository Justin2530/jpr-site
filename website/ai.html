Questions

Good to know.

Do we have to talk to a salesperson?

No. The calls are with our AI assistant, and your workbook comes by email. If you want us to build something, you can reach out when you're ready.

Who should be on the calls?

Anyone whose day you want to understand: the owner, the office manager, dispatch, the people answering phones. Each person takes their own short call.

Are the calls recorded?

Yes, so the answers can be written up accurately. The assistant asks each person for permission at the start of the call, and anyone can decline.

What happens to our information?

It's used only to build your workbook. We don't sell it or share it.

Do we have to buy anything after?

No. The workbook is yours to use however you like.
