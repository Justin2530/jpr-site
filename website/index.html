We do more than post the job and wait.

Finding the right person often means finding people who aren't actively applying to your opening.

JPR searches for candidates whose experience matches what you're looking for, reaches out directly and speaks with interested candidates before introducing them to you.

01 — Search

We identify potential candidates based on the position, experience, location and requirements that matter to you.

02 — Outreach

We contact candidates directly to determine whether they're interested in hearing about the opportunity.

03 — Screening

We discuss their experience, compensation expectations, availability and the questions that matter for your position.

04 — Introduction

You receive the candidate's resume along with the relevant information from our screening. From there, you decide who you'd like to interview.
