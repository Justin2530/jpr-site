Manufacturing & Machining Recruiting, Nationwide

Manufacturing is where JPR started. While our everyday work is helping employers around Punxsutawney fill all kinds of positions, we still recruit manufacturing and machining talent for companies anywhere in the U.S.

If your shop or plant needs skilled people and the applications aren't coming in, we go find them: people with the right machines, materials and experience, most of whom aren't looking at job postings.

Positions we recruit

  • CNC machinists, setup machinists and operators
  • CNC programmers (Mastercam, Fusion, G-code)
  • Tool and die makers, machine builders and fabricators
  • Welders and fitters
  • Maintenance technicians and electricians
  • Quality inspectors, technicians and engineers
  • Manufacturing, process and production engineers
  • Production supervisors, plant managers and operations leaders

And more. If it's a direct-hire role on the manufacturing side, ask us.

How it works

Search. We identify people whose experience fits your machines, parts, tolerances and shift.

Outreach. We contact them directly to see who's interested in hearing about your opening.

Screening. We talk through their experience, pay expectations, availability and willingness to relocate when it applies.

Introduction. You get the resume plus what we learned. You decide who to interview and hire.
