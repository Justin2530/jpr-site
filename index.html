How it works

You tell us who you need. We do the rest.

  1. A 15-minute call about the role

    Pay, shift, must-haves, and what the last good person in the job looked like.

  2. We find and talk to candidates

    We search, reach out, and have a real conversation with every interested person about experience, pay, schedule, and start date.

  3. You get ready-to-interview candidates

    Each one arrives with a resume and clear notes on what they told us. No stack of random applications.

  4. You interview and hire

    You make the call on who gets hired. On pay-per-hire, you pay nothing until they start.

Interested candidates hear back from us within minutes, not days. Good people don't wait around.
