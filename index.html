How JPR works

The people you want may not be looking. So we go looking for them.

We don't post your job and wait. We search for the right people, reach out to them directly, and have the first conversation, so you spend less time chasing applicants and more time talking to potential hires.

  1. Tell us who you need

    The position, the requirements, the pay, the schedule, and what actually matters in the person. One conversation.

  2. We go find them

    We search, reach out, and talk with interested people. We find out their experience, what they're looking for, and what it would take to make a move.

  3. You interview and hire

    We send candidates straight to you with the details you need. You decide who to meet and who to hire.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What kinds of positions do you recruit for?

Permanent roles in almost every industry, from skilled trades and manufacturing to accounting, engineering, sales, healthcare, and leadership.

Do you provide temporary workers?

No. We specialize in permanent direct-hire recruiting. Your new hire is your employee from day one. There's no temporary staffing and no payroll markup.

How much does it cost?

Either 15% of first-year base salary, earned when your hire starts and due Net 15, or $2,500 per month with a six-month minimum for ongoing recruiting.

Is there a guarantee?

Placement fees do not include a replacement or retention guarantee. The fee covers the search, recruitment, and successful placement of your new hire.

Can you recruit confidentially?

Yes. If you're replacing someone or keeping a search quiet, we can recruit without naming your company until the right time.

What area do you cover?

We're based in Punxsutawney and focus on employers throughout Western and Central Pennsylvania. We also take on searches outside the region when it makes sense for the client.
